02 · Color

Near-black, soft whites and one violet.

Violet is the only accent: links, highlights, labels, buttons, text selection. Never use it for large text blocks. Click a swatch to copy its value.

03 · Typography

Two families. One speaks, one labels.

Instrument Sans for titles and text, always weight 500 for headings with tight negative tracking. Space Mono only for labels, buttons and small details: always uppercase, tracking 0.08em.

06 · Voice

Short, direct, in first person.

English across the site. Headlines are a single idea with the key words in accent.

Do

  • Say what I do and how: "From design to code, without handoffs."
  • Keep headings under eight words.
  • Use real project facts instead of adjectives.

Don't

  • Stack buzzwords like "innovative cutting-edge solutions".
  • Use accent on whole sentences or paragraphs.
  • Mix in other typefaces or colors.
